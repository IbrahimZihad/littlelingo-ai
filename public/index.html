A NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS

Caregiver Information

What is LittleLingo AI?

LittleLingo AI is a playful English practice tool for young learners. Buddy offers short conversations, vocabulary cards, quizzes, and feelings vocabulary.

Caregiver account

Your caregiver account stores progress securely under your account. Child profiles use random IDs; please do not enter a child’s real name.

Privacy and data

  • No account or real name is required. A random learner ID is linked to the caregiver account.
  • Progress records store level, quiz attempts, stars, and activity counts in the configured database.
  • Chat messages are sent to this app's server. If Gemini is configured, a message and limited learning context may be sent to Google for a response.
  • Microphone speech recognition is browser-dependent. This app does not intentionally save raw microphone recordings.
  • Do not enter personal details. Adult supervision is recommended.

Important limitations

This educational app is not a babysitter, therapist, or emergency service. Its safety checks are limited and do not establish legal or regulatory compliance. Caregivers should review the privacy rules applicable to their location before deployment.
